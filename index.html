/ How I think

i.

Teams commit to outcomes when they understand what those outcomes will actually mean for them.

Before any work starts, I make sure everyone in the room understands not just where we're going, but what it's going to ask of them. That's what turns agreement into commitment.

ii.

Teams that fixate on what they can't control burn out. It also stops them from moving what they can.

Stuck usually isn't stuck. It's misplaced focus. I help the team find what's actually in reach, and that gets people moving again.

iii.

Hard problems don't go away when you minimize them. They become manageable when you give them room to be hard.

Some of my best work has been on problems we haven't finished solving yet. Keeping a team honest, engaged, and willing to try again. That fuels learning, progress, and innovation.
